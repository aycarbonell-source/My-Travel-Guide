Culture and Traditions

Filipino culture is influenced by Malay, Spanish, Chinese, and American traditions. Filipinos celebrate colorful festivals such as Sinulog, Ati-Atihan, and Pahiyas. Family gatherings, respect for elders, and religious celebrations are important traditions. Popular Filipino values include bayanihan (helping one another), utang na loob (gratitude), and pakikisama (getting along with others).
